# Credits & Usage

AI generation is metered by **credits**. Each generation consumes a number of credits depending on the provider, media type and quality level.

---

## Checking your balance

Your current credit balance is shown as a badge on the **AI Studio** tab when you are in Media Studio.

---

## Credit consumption

| Generation type | Quality | Approximate credits |
|----------------|---------|---------------------|
| Image | Preview | Low |
| Image | Standard | Medium |
| Image | High | High |
| Video (short) | Standard | High |
| Video (long) | Standard | Very high |

Exact costs vary by provider. The number of credits consumed for each generation is shown in the **Generation Log**.

!!! warning "Credits are non-refundable"
    Credits are consumed when you click Generate, regardless of whether you save or discard the result.

---

## Generation Log

Go to **Media Studio → Generation Log** to see a full history of all generations for your client.

Each row shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Thumbnail | Preview image or video |
| Prompt | The prompt used |
| Provider | Which AI provider was used |
| Quality | Quality level selected |
| Credits | Credits consumed |
| Date | When it was generated |
| Status | Saved / Discarded / Pending |

---

## Credit allocation (Superadmin)

Superadmins allocate credits to each client from the **SA Dashboard**:

1. Go to the SA Dashboard from the Home quick-links
2. Find the **AI Credits** panel
3. Set a monthly allocation per client
4. Click **Save**

Credits reset at the start of each billing period.

---

## Running low

When your balance is low, a warning appears in AI Studio. Contact your Superadmin to request a top-up or increased allocation.
