# Generating Videos

AI Studio supports video generation through providers that offer text-to-video capability.

---

## Availability

Not all providers support video. The provider dropdown only shows providers that are configured and enabled for your client. If no video providers appear, contact your Superadmin.

---

## Generating a video

1. Go to **Media Studio → AI Studio**
2. Select **Text → Video** as the generation type
3. Choose a **provider** — only video-capable providers are listed
4. Enter your **prompt** describing the scene or motion
5. Select **duration** and **aspect ratio** (options vary by provider)
6. Click **Generate**

Video generation typically takes **30–120 seconds** depending on the provider and settings. A progress indicator is shown while you wait.

---

## Prompt tips for video

Video prompts benefit from describing **motion and time**:

> "A slow pan across a busy city skyline at dusk, smooth camera movement, cinematic"
> "Looping abstract geometric shapes morphing, vibrant colours, clean background"

Keep prompts short and focused — overly complex prompts often produce unpredictable results.

---

## Saving a video

Once generated, the video preview appears inline. Click **Save to Library** to add it to your client's Media Library as a permanent asset.

!!! warning "Video credits"
    Video generation uses significantly more credits than images. Check your credit balance before generating.

---

## Using videos in playlists

Saved videos appear in your Media Library. You can use them in:

- A **Cinema Carousel** widget (video slide)
- A **Video** widget (single full-screen video)
