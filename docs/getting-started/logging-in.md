# Logging In

## Accessing the admin dashboard

Open your browser and go to the PlayVisual admin URL provided by your system administrator.

The login page asks for:

- **Email address** — the address your account was registered with
- **Password** — set when your account was created or last changed

Click **Sign In**.

!!! tip "Forgot your password?"
    Contact your administrator to reset your password. Self-service password reset is not currently available.

---

## After logging in

You are redirected to the **Home** dashboard. What you see depends on your role:

- **Superadmin** — full navigation including Clients, SA Dashboard and all admin tools
- **Admin** — navigation showing Devices, Users, Playlists, Media Studio and Settings
- **Moderator** — navigation showing Playlists and limited media access

---

## Language

PlayVisual supports multiple languages. Click the language selector (flag icon) in the top navbar to switch. Your preference is saved per session.

---

## Logging out

Click **LogOut** in the top-right corner of the navbar.

!!! warning
    Sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity. If you see the login page unexpectedly, your session has timed out — log in again.
